When someone searches "best cafe near me" or "gym in Gurgaon," Google shows the Map Pack. Three businesses. Click-to-call. Directions. Reviews.

If you're not in that pack, you don't exist.

The challenge for Delhi NCR businesses: the Map Pack is brutally competitive, especially in saturated categories like restaurants, dentists, lawyers, and real estate. Here's what actually works in 2025.

## The Map Pack ranking factors (current)

Based on our analysis of 200+ Delhi NCR businesses:

1. **Google Business Profile optimisation** — still the single biggest factor (~30% weight)
2. **Reviews velocity and quality** — both quantity AND recency matter (~25%)
3. **Proximity to searcher** — you can't change this, but you can dominate your specific area
4. **On-page local signals** — NAP consistency, local content, schema markup (~20%)
5. **Behavioural signals** — click-through rate, calls, direction requests (~15%)
6. **Citation quality** — yes, still matters (~10%)

## The framework we use

### Step 1: GBP overhaul (Week 1)

Most businesses treat Google Business Profile as a directory listing. Wrong. It's a landing page.

- **Categories**: Use every relevant category. Primary + 4-5 secondary.
- **Description**: 750 characters, keyword-rich, written for humans AND ranking.
- **Photos**: Weekly uploads. Real photos, not stock. Owner, staff, customers, products.
- **Posts**: Weekly Google Posts. Offers, events, updates, what's new.
- **Q&A**: Seed with your own questions and answers. Pre-empt common queries.
- **Services/Menu**: Complete with descriptions. Use every character.
- **Attributes**: Every applicable attribute. Accessibility, payment, amenities.

### Step 2: Review engine (Ongoing)

The single biggest mistake: asking for reviews once and giving up.

- **Velocity matters more than total count.** 5 reviews/month for 12 months beats 60 reviews once.
- **Response to every review, positive or negative.** Owners who respond get 35% more reviews.
- **Use keywords naturally in review responses.** "Best Italian restaurant in Hauz Khas" beats "Thank you".
- **Diversify review platforms.** Google is critical, but JustDial, Practo, Zomato, and industry-specific platforms add signals.

### Step 3: Local content hub (Week 2-4)

Build neighbourhood authority:

- **Location pages** for every area you serve (Connaught Place, Saket, Gurgaon Sector 29, etc.)
- **Local guides** — "The best coworking spaces near Cyber Hub"
- **Local event coverage** — sponsor, attend, write about local happenings
- **Case studies** featuring local clients (with permission)

### Step 4: Citation & NAP cleanup (Week 1)

- **Audit existing citations** for NAP (Name, Address, Phone) inconsistencies.
- **Build citations on tier-1 platforms** — JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, Yelp, Apple Maps.
- **Industry-specific citations** — Practo for doctors, Zomato for restaurants, MagicBricks for real estate.

### Step 5: Behavioural signal optimisation (Ongoing)

- **Photos in GBP**: businesses with 100+ photos get 520% more calls than average.
- **Click-through rate**: write titles and meta descriptions that earn clicks.
- **Q&A engagement**: respond quickly to questions.
- **Posts**: businesses posting weekly get 30% more direction requests.

## The technical SEO layer

Local SEO without technical SEO is a house on sand. Make sure:

- **Schema markup**: LocalBusiness, Organization, Review, FAQ schema on every page.
- **Mobile performance**: 90+ Lighthouse score. Most local searches are mobile.
- **Page speed**: under 2 seconds. Every 100ms costs conversions.
- **NAP on every page**: footer with full address, phone, embedded map.

## The competition reality

In Delhi NCR, you're competing against:

- Aggregator sites (JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha)
- National chains with bigger budgets
- Other local businesses finally waking up to local SEO

The opportunity: most local businesses still treat GBP as a set-and-forget listing. Doing the basics with discipline puts you ahead of 80% of competitors.

## Measuring success

Track weekly:

- GBP insights: views, clicks, calls, direction requests
- Map Pack rankings for your target keywords
- Review count, average rating, response rate
- Phone calls and form submissions attributed to GBP

For one client (a dental clinic in Gurgaon), this exact framework moved them from invisible to Map Pack #1 for 14 high-intent keywords in 8 months. New patient bookings up 220%.

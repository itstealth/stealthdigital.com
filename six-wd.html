FAQ

Got Questions? Let’s Clear Things Up.

How long does a typical web design project take with six2eight?

Most web design projects take 3 to 5 weeks from start to finish. The exact timeline depends on your content readiness, feedback speed, and scope. We work in weekly sprints to keep things moving fast without cutting corners. If you’re launching something urgent, we offer accelerated delivery options. Our process ensures steady progress, clear updates, and no endless delays. Every phase is planned to minimize back-and-forth and keep you in the loop.

Do I need to provide content for my website, or will you handle it?

You can provide content or let us handle it for you. We offer copywriting support if you need help with messaging. Our team works with you to capture your tone, audience, and goals. If you already have content, we’ll guide edits to make it web-friendly. For new pages, we structure and write based on UX best practices. Whether it’s just headlines or full pages, we’ve got your back.

What platforms do you design websites for?

We design for all major platforms, including Webflow, WordPress, and Shopify. We also support custom-built solutions. Our designs are flexible, responsive, and optimized for whichever CMS or platform you use. If you’re unsure which one to pick, we’ll help you choose based on your goals and budget. From no-code to full-stack setups, we design with scalability in mind. Your tech stack doesn’t limit our creative process.

Can you design a website that integrates with my tools or CRM?

Yes, we design sites that work seamlessly with CRMs, marketing tools, and third-party platforms. We plan integrations early to ensure smooth functionality. Whether it’s HubSpot, Mailchimp, Airtable, or something custom, we’ve worked with it. Our goal is to make sure your design doesn’t break your workflows. We collaborate with your dev or marketing team if needed. Your website won’t just look good, it’ll play nice with your tools.

What happens after the design is done? Do you offer support or development?

After the design phase, we can support development or hand off assets to your team. We prep all files for a smooth handoff. If you need full development, we’ve got dedicated developers ready. We also offer launch support to make sure nothing breaks live. Post-launch, we can help with design tweaks or performance audits. You’re never left hanging with half-finished assets.

Can you design websites for SaaS or B2B companies?

Absolutely. We specialize in SaaS and B2B web design that focuses on clarity, trust, and conversions. We understand how different B2B buyers think. Our process includes research into your product and market positioning. We simplify technical content and structure complex offerings with a clear UX. The end result is a website that builds trust and shortens the sales cycle. We’ve helped many SaaS founders go from landing page to full product website.

How much does a website design project typically cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, complexity, and add-ons like copy or dev handoff. Most projects start around $3,000. We offer flat pricing so you know exactly what you’re paying for. Every quote includes milestones, a timeline, and deliverables. There are no surprise charges or hourly invoices. If you’re scaling or need something leaner, we adjust the scope without losing quality. You’ll get high-quality design matched to your budget.
